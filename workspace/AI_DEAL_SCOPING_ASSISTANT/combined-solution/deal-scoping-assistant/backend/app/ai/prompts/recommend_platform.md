Stage: recommend one cloud platform.

Choose between `aws`, `azure` and `gcp` using the customer's technology preferences,
existing systems, integrations, data residency, security and compliance requirements and
delivery constraints.

- `recommended`: the platform you propose.
- `rationale`: why it fits this customer, referencing the requirements that drive the
  choice. No generic vendor marketing.
- `alternatives`: the two other platforms, each with a short, factual `why_not`.
- `req_ids`: the scope ids that drove the decision.
- `basis`: `customer` when the customer stated a preference, otherwise `recommended`,
  and then set `validation_required` with a reason.
