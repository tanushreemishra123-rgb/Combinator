Stage: write the executive summary of the scoping package.

You receive the generated deliverables. Summarise them for a sales or delivery lead.

- `overview`: one paragraph on the customer's situation and the proposed solution.
- `customer_objectives`: what the customer wants to achieve, with the BR ids.
- `solution_highlights`: the few points that matter (scope, platform, data and
  integration approach, AI approach), each with the ids behind it.
- `key_risks`: the risks and open questions a reviewer must see before sending anything
  to the customer.
- `next_steps`: what the team should do next, for example answering open questions or
  confirming assumptions.

Write no numbers about effort, cost, duration, dates or commercials anywhere. The
application adds the commercial paragraph from its own calculation.
