Product or solution overview

Meridian Assurance Group needs its legacy .NET/Oracle ClaimsDesk portal replaced with a cloud-native claims platform on Azure, integrated with Guidewire ClaimCenter, SAP S/4HANA and Okta, serving roughly 242,000 internal and policyholder users within a 46-week window; the SAP integration pattern and the phasing of the policyholder status tracker remain open. (Refs: BR_01, BR_02, BR_03, BR_04, BR_05; AI interpretation)

ClaimsDesk Modernization Program serves 5 business objectives set by Meridian Assurance Group (BR_01, BR_02, BR_03, BR_04 and BR_05); each is detailed under Business objectives. (Refs: BR_01, BR_02, BR_03, BR_04, BR_05; Customer-requested)

The solution is organised into 6 capability areas (Application Experience, Platform Foundation, Data Platform, Systems Integration, AI Capabilities and Security and Compliance) and connects to Guidewire ClaimCenter (SYS_01), SAP S/4HANA (SYS_02), Okta (SYS_03) and Hyland OnBase (SYS_05). (Refs: SYS_01, SYS_02, SYS_03, SYS_05; AI interpretation)

Business problem

Today Meridian Assurance Group relies on Legacy ClaimsDesk Portal: The current claims intake and adjudication system, built in 2011 on .NET Framework 4.8 with an Oracle 19c database, being replaced by this program. (Refs: SYS_04; Customer-requested)

Until this is addressed, Meridian Assurance Group cannot achieve "Reduce claim-intake processing time" (BR_01), "Unified claim, policy and payment view for examiners" (BR_02), "Improve first-contact resolution for simple claims" (BR_03), "Enable direct agent claim submission" (BR_04) and "Policyholder claim-status tracker (candidate)" (BR_05). (Refs: BR_01, BR_02, BR_03, BR_04, BR_05; Customer-requested)

Calculation rules

RuleNameFormulaDescription
CR_01Driver effortdriver days = baseEffort[driver type][complexity]; complexity = reviewer override ?? AI suggestionEach scope driver is sized from the editable base-effort table. The AI only suggests the complexity level; a reviewer override replaces it and is marked user-set. A driver without a base-effort value contributes no days, is reported as a blocked scope driver and blocks the cost of its workstream and the ROM.
CR_02Workstream base effort and complexitybase days = sum(driver days); complexity = round(sum(level x driver days) / sum(driver days)), Low = 1 ... Very High = 4A workstream is the sum of its drivers. Its complexity is the effort-weighted average of the driver complexity levels.
CR_03Environment upliftinfrastructure days x (1 + max(0, environments - baselineEnvironments) x extraEnvironmentPct / 100)Environments beyond the baseline add effort to infrastructure drivers only. When the number of environments is unknown, no uplift is applied, the input is listed as missing and confidence is reduced.
CR_04Productivity factorlikely days = round((non-infrastructure days + uplifted infrastructure days) x productivityFactor)The productivity assumption scales all build effort (below 1 is faster than baseline). Likely effort is rounded to whole days.
CR_05Overhead phasesphase days = round(total likely build days x pctOfBuild / 100)Discovery and Architecture, Testing and Hardening, and Deployment and Handover are sized as configurable percentages of the adjusted build effort and staffed with the overhead role mix.
CR_06Workstream confidenceHigh -> Medium (Very High driver, related open gap or question, outstanding delivery dependency, missing planning input) -> Low (critical unresolved gap or question on an affected input, or a blocked calculation)Inputs map to workstreams by driver type: integration readiness -> integration, data volume -> data, AI data availability -> AI, expected users / environments / target regions -> infrastructure, compliance scope -> security, delivery deadline -> timeline. Every reduction is listed as a confidence limitation. Overhead phases inherit the overall confidence.
CR_07Overall confidenceLow if any calculation is blocked, Low-confidence workstreams hold >= 30% of build effort, or 4 or more distinct estimation inputs are missing; Medium if any workstream is below High or any input is missing; else HighThe overall level is weighted by effort so that one small uncertain workstream does not dominate, while blocked calculations and a largely unspecified engagement always do.
CR_08Effort rangelow = round(likely x (1 - lowPct / 100)); high = round(likely x (1 + highPct / 100))The range width comes from the confidence-spread table, so lower confidence widens the range instead of presenting unsupported precision. Totals are the sums of the workstream ranges.
